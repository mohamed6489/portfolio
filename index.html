Factory HR System

HR and payroll for a factory of 50 to 200 workers, built to replace the Excel sheets the HR office runs on. It is in a trial run at the factory now. It costs nothing to host: the backend runs on Google Apps Script and the data stays in the HR's own Google Sheet.

My roleDeveloper, from the written spec to the desktop build and the handover guide.

  • Attendance imported from the fingerprint device, reviewed before it is saved
  • Payroll with payslips, a bank file and month locking, on Egyptian Labor Law 14/2025 defaults
  • One set of business rules shared by the browser demo, Apps Script and a desktop app

  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind
